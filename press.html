N3mak Exposure Index

A free, machine-updated measure of how many companies in each country have been published by ransomware groups. It is rebuilt every six hours by an autonomous agent from public leak-site listings.

Method

Each signal is a company name that a ransomware group published on its own leak site, taken from public aggregators. The index for a country is its share of published victims, scaled so the busiest country in the current window reads 100. Levels: critical above 66, elevated above 33, watch below.

Limits, stated plainly: only companies that were published are counted, so quiet incidents are invisible. Names follow whatever the group posted. It is not a penetration test and it never claims a breach. Figures move with the six-hour rebuild.

Why it exists

Small businesses rarely learn that their name is on a leak site until a client mentions it. The index makes that public and countable, and gives every country a number that can be quoted.
