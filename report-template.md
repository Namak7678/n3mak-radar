2. Contact the company's authorized security/IT lead through a verified channel.
3. Review identity-provider sign-ins, MFA, privileged accounts, endpoint alerts, and backups.
4. Preserve logs and evidence; engage a qualified incident-response provider if there are internal indicators.
5. Avoid paying, negotiating, downloading stolen files, or contacting threat actors based on this report.
